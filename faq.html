Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our training programs, enrollment process, and what makes Arvion unique.

Can I cancel my booking and get a refund?

Yes. Programs can be rescheduled with at least 7 days' notice. Cancellations more than 14 days before the start date are eligible for a full refund; later cancellations may receive credit toward a future program. Terms are confirmed in your agreement.

How long does it take to receive my refund?

Once approved, refunds are typically processed within 7–14 business days, depending on your payment method and bank. We will confirm by email when the refund has been initiated.

What happens if Arvion cancels the service or event?

If we need to cancel a session, you can choose a full refund or reschedule at no extra cost. We will notify you as soon as possible and help you pick a new date that works for your team.

What kind of training does Arvion provide?

Arvion offers Corporate Training, Leadership Programs, Technology Training, and Soft Skills development. Each track can be tailored to your industry, team size, and goals.

Do you provide certificates?

Yes. Participants receive a certificate of completion after finishing a program. Self-certified learning credits are available; formal accreditation is in progress.

Can I join as a trainer?

We welcome experienced industry professionals and educators. Email hello@arvion.com with your background and specialties, and our team will follow up about joining the Arvion trainer network.

How do I register for a course?

Click Enroll Now or Contact Us, or email hello@arvion.com. We will help you choose a track, scope a custom program if needed, and schedule delivery for your team.

Are your courses customizable for organizations?

Yes. We design programs around your specific goals, industry requirements, and employee skill needs — including custom content, flexible delivery, and onsite or virtual formats.

What is the duration of your training programs?

Most workshops run from one intensive day to several weeks. Duration depends on the topic, cohort size, and whether you want a short sprint or a longer learning path.

Do you offer training for beginners as well as advanced learners?

Yes. Programs are leveled for beginners through advanced practitioners. We assess your team's starting point and tailor depth, pace, and examples so everyone can progress.

What delivery methods do you use?

Sessions are available in person, virtually, or as a hybrid. Your team can learn together in one office or across locations, with the same practical, hands-on approach.

How soon can I start after enrolling?

Timing depends on program type and trainer availability. Many standard workshops can start within a few weeks; custom corporate programs are scheduled after a short discovery call.

Do you offer any post-training support?

Yes. Many programs include follow-up resources, check-ins, or coaching so learning sticks on the job. Ask about post-training options when you enroll — we will match support to your goals.

Still Have Questions?

Can't find the answer you're looking for? Our support team is here to help you with any specific questions about our training programs.
